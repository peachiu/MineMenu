MineMeBoost 3.5.0 for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1** (Fabric): sharper Render Scale, Impostors for far mobs, presets that learn from your own A/B test, a schematics browser, Undo for vanilla structures, and the whole mod in **Portuguese (Portugal)**.

## MineBoost tab (2.5)

- **Sharpen (CAS) for Render Scale**: the smaller world picture is now stretched with Contrast Adaptive Sharpening (AMD FidelityFX CAS) in the same pass, so 75% looks close to 100% (edge contrast in our test: +17% at 50% strength). Render Scale 75% with Sharpen still gave **+28% FPS** in our A/B test; on a CPU renderer the sharpening itself costs up to 18%, on a graphics card a fraction of a millisecond (set it Off for maximum FPS). Strength 0-100%, 50% by default.
- **Impostors**: mobs farther than 16-64 blocks are drawn as one flat picture of themselves (each kind photographed once from 8 sides) instead of the full model with armor and items. **+11% FPS and +53% 1% low** with 150 armored mobs in view. Off by default: far mobs don't animate.
- **Presets use your A/B results**: run Tools → A/B test once, and Toaster, Potato and Intel iGPU pick the Render Scale that really gave FPS on *your* PC (Toaster 50% or 75%, Potato and Intel iGPU 75% or 100%). Without an A/B test only Toaster lowers it (75%). The preset's tooltip says which scale and why.
- The A/B test now also measures Impostors and the cost of Sharpen.

## MineMenu tab (3.5)

- **Schematics browser** (Build → Schematics...): your saved schematics with a search box, size and block count, and two pictures (from above and from the front). Load & paste, rename, delete (asks twice), save a copy, open the folder.
- **Undo for vanilla structures**: villages, temples, igloos, outposts... placed from Structures... can be undone. Every block a structure changes is remembered while it's being placed (in our test, an igloo changed 456 blocks and Undo put all of them back). Mobs that came with it stay.

## Português (Portugal)

Every text in MineMenu, MineBoost and MineMeBoost now comes from the language files (`assets/*/lang/en_us.json`), and a full **Portuguese (Portugal)** translation is included: 1,124 texts. Pick **Português (Portugal)** in Options → Language. The menu's buttons get wider when the words are longer.

## Behind the scenes

- An **in-game test suite** (`./gradlew runClientTests`, in `minemeboost/src/clienttest`, never part of the mod jar) drives a real Minecraft client with the real jar: every hack, tool and screen, PASS/FAIL lines and screenshots. 87 checks.
- **Actions → In-game tests** on GitHub runs it plus a **soak test** (15, 30 or 60 minutes of Chaos Mode, Minimap, ESP, Impostors, fills, morphs, meteors...) that fails on any error caught by Safe Mode or on steady memory growth. It also runs every Monday with a 60-minute soak.

## Install

1. Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.21.1) and **`minemeboost-3.5.0.jar`** (below) in your `.minecraft/mods` folder. Remove the old `minemeboost` jar, and any `minemenu-*.jar` or `mineboost-*.jar`.
3. Your settings carry over (`config/mineboost.json` gets `configVersion` 2; nothing is lost).
