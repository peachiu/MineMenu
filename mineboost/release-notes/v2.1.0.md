MineBoost 2.1.0 for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1** (Fabric): Smart Culling, Auto Preset and a Toaster preset.

- **Smart Culling** (on by default): mobs, items, chests and signs hidden behind solid blocks aren't drawn. Glass and leaves never hide anything, and things near you and glowing mobs are always drawn.
- **Auto Preset:** a new button that tries the presets from Ultra down and keeps the best looking one that reaches your FPS Target (or 60 FPS). The result is posted in chat.
- **Toaster preset:** even lighter than Potato (2 chunk view distance).

How much Smart Culling helps depends on how much is hidden behind walls: in our test, 100 mobs sealed in a stone box went from 100 drawn to 0 drawn. On the test PC (no graphics card) the FPS difference was too small to measure reliably, so we're not promising a number.

## Install

1. Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.21.1) and **`mineboost-2.1.0.jar`** (below) in your `.minecraft/mods` folder. Remove the old `mineboost` jar.
3. Join a world and press **`.`**.

Want MineMenu too? Get **MineMeBoost 3.1.0** instead (don't install both).
