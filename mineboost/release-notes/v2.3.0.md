MineBoost 2.3.0 for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1** (Fabric): measured FPS gains, an Intel iGPU preset and compatibility with other FPS mods.

- **Intel iGPU preset**: made for laptops with Intel UHD / Iris graphics. No menu blur, clouds, Fancy leaves or shadows; keeps smooth lighting and 8 chunks.
- **Simple Far Mobs**: armor, held items and other extra layers aren't drawn on far mobs. **+31% FPS, +48% 1% low** with 98 armored zombies in view (+13% on top of Sodium).
- **Idle Mode**: lower FPS after 15 s to 5 min without input; full FPS the moment you touch anything. Off by default.
- **Max Particles: the closest particles win** when the limit is reached.
- **Benchmark compares with your last run** and lists the settings you changed in between.
- **Every tooltip has an "Impact:" line** with the measured gain. All numbers and the method: [docs/fps-measurements.md](https://github.com/peachiu/MineMenu/blob/mineboost-v2.3.0/mineboost/docs/fps-measurements.md).
- **Works with Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast and EntityCulling**: detected and shown on screen; Smart Culling switches off with EntityCulling, the vanilla Chunk Builder setting is hidden with Sodium. Tested with all five at once.

Measured and left out because they made no difference: Smooth Chunk Loading, Animation LOD, fog off.

## Install

1. Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.21.1) and **`mineboost-2.3.0.jar`** (below) in your `.minecraft/mods` folder, replacing the old MineBoost jar. Your `config/mineboost.json` carries over.
