MineBoost 2.4.0 for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1** (Fabric): Render Scale, a Lag Doctor, an A/B test of every boost and more tools.

- **Render Scale**: the world at 50-99% resolution, HUD and menus sharp. **+15% FPS at 50%** on our test PC (+17% at 75% in a busy scene). **Dynamic Render Scale** lowers it only below your FPS target. Off with Iris and with Fabulous graphics.
- **Lag Doctor** (Tools...): what costs you FPS, with one-click fixes; frames over 50 ms logged to `config/minemeboost/spikes.log`.
- **A/B test of every boost** on your PC, saved to `config/minemeboost/benchmark-results.md`.
- **Memory panel + JVM Advisor**, **context rules** (Nether, underground, elytra...), shareable **FPS profiles**, **frame-time graph**, **Battery Saver**, **Max Sounds**, **Smooth Fog Edge**.
- Smart Culling's 3-second hitch fixed; particle and entity budgets use primitive collections.
- Measured and **off by default** (no gain): Section Culling (−7%), Far Entity Ticks (−2%). All numbers: [fps-measurements.md](https://github.com/peachiu/MineMenu/blob/mineboost-v2.4.0/mineboost/docs/fps-measurements.md).
- Tested with Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, Iris and Mod Menu.

## Install

1. Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.21.1) and **`mineboost-2.4.0.jar`** (below) in your `.minecraft/mods` folder, replacing the old MineBoost jar. Your `config/mineboost.json` carries over.
