MineBoost 2.5.0 for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1** (Fabric): sharper Render Scale, Impostors for far mobs, presets that learn from your own A/B test, and Portuguese (Portugal).

- **Sharpen (CAS) for Render Scale**: the smaller world picture is stretched with Contrast Adaptive Sharpening in the same pass, so 75% looks close to 100%. Render Scale 75% with Sharpen gave **+28% FPS** in our A/B test. Strength 0-100% (50% by default; Off for maximum FPS).
- **Impostors**: mobs farther than 16-64 blocks are drawn as one flat picture of themselves (photographed from 8 sides). **+11% FPS and +53% 1% low** with 150 armored mobs in view. Off by default (far mobs don't animate). Off with Iris.
- **Presets use your A/B results**: after Tools → A/B test, Toaster, Potato and Intel iGPU pick the Render Scale that really gave FPS on your PC; the tooltip says which and why.
- The A/B test also measures Impostors and the cost of Sharpen.
- **Português (Portugal)**: every text is in the language files, with a full Portuguese translation.
- All numbers: [fps-measurements.md](https://github.com/peachiu/MineMenu/blob/mineboost-v2.5.0/mineboost/docs/fps-measurements.md).

## Install

1. Install the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (1.21.1) and **`mineboost-2.5.0.jar`** (below) in your `.minecraft/mods` folder, replacing the old MineBoost jar. Your `config/mineboost.json` carries over.
